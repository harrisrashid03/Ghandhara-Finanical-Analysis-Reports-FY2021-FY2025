# 🚚 Financial Analysis of Ghandhara Automobiles Limited (GAL)
### FY2021–FY2025 | Ratio & Trend Analysis

A ratio and trend analysis of GAL's consolidated financial performance, built from its audited Consolidated Financial Statements and PSX data.

## 🏢 About GAL

PSX-listed assembler of JAC trucks/pick-ups and importer of Dongfeng & Renault vehicles, with a 19% equity stake in Ghandhara Industries Limited (Isuzu assembler).

## 🔍 Approach

- **📐 Ratio Analysis** — Liquidity, Activity, Leverage, Profitability, DuPont, Investor/Market Value, Free Cash Flow
- **📊 Trend Analysis** — Vertical (common-size) & horizontal (base-year) analysis of BS, IS, and CF

## 💡 Key Findings

- 📈 **Profitability hit 5-year highs in FY2025** (net margin, ROA, ROE, ROCE), after a FY2023 dip caused by higher finance cost, an associate loss, and a high tax rate
- 🧩 **DuPont** shows ROE growth (1.7% → 31.9%) came from margin & efficiency gains, not added leverage
- 🛡️ **Debt stayed low and was paid down**, even as the balance sheet grew — expansion was funded by customer advances, not borrowing
- 💧 **Liquidity weakened** — quick/cash ratios dipped below 1.0x most years
- ⚠️ **Operating cash flow went negative** in FY2023 & FY2024 despite positive profit
- 🎯 **FY2025 is an exceptional peak** — driven by a large customer-advances inflow and an outsized associate contribution, flagged as unlikely to repeat at the same scale
